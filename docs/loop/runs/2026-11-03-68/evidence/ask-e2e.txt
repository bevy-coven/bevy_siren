## The floor is too aggressive, and this is the measurement that says so

At **0.126** — the weakest correct match measured in iteration 63 — the floor drops **2, 2, 1 and 3** of 3 results. On
the last query it drops **everything**, because 0.126 is the *lowest* score a correct match happened to reach, not a
threshold any correct match clears.

That is why `Floor`'s default stays **0.0** and says so in its doc comment: a floor picked from the bottom of the
correct band removes most of the answer. `V-01`'s labelled set is what would choose a defensible number, and until it
exists a long list a caller can weigh beats a short list that has silently dropped the answer.

**The two near-ties are honest.** `white noise hiss` puts `rain.wav` (0.429) ahead of `noise.wav` (0.423) — a 0.006 gap
between two clips both generated from white noise. `rain falling` makes the same call from the other side. Neither is a
failure of the seam; both are the model saying two things are nearly the same.
