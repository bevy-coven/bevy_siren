Cause (read, not yet fixed): src/model.rs:305-308 `Rng::new(seed)` uses the seed AS the xorshift64 state, unscrambled, and `next_u32` (:317-324) returns the TOP 32 bits of the state after one xorshift step; for a seed far below 2^60 those bits are
a small multiple of the seed, so the first draw is ~ seed/16 and the first pick (`floor(draw x variants)`) is variant 0. The README's *"two seeds are two streams from the first play"* holds for the stream and not for its first play: every small seed - the natural thing to
write: `seed: 42` - starts on the first variant at a pitch the seed sets. The default seed (0x5EED_51AE_0000_0001) is a large constant and unaffected.
