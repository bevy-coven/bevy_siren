## The bug this found, which is the reason to pin ids rather than words

`falling` alone tokenises to `[7349, 154]` — `fall` + `ing`, two pieces, because the merge table has `(fall, ing)` at
rank 7207. But **` falling`, with a leading space, is one token: `[3064]`**, because `Ġfalling` is a single vocabulary
entry reached by the chain `(Ġ,f) (Ġf,a) …`.

So the same word is one token or two **depending on whether a space precedes it**. A tokeniser that split on whitespace
and looked each word up would produce `[7349, 154]` for `rain falling` and the wrong embedding for every phrase in a
description — and nothing downstream would fail.

**Both cases are pinned:** `a_word_that_splits_is_not_looked_up_whole` asserts `tense` is two pieces, and the reference
table asserts `rain falling` is `[9946, 3064]`.

## A second bug, in the parser

`tokenizer.json` writes `":"` and `","` as `"\":\": 6`. Splitting each entry on the first `:` takes `"` as the key and
fails on the whole file. And `"vocab"` appears twice — inside `model` and inside `added_tokens` — so a plain `find`
picks the wrong one. Both are fixed by reading the quoted key first and scanning for the key at a known brace depth.
