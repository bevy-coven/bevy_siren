## Measurement (mono 48 kHz WAV, Mode::I | Mode::TRUE_PEAK)

    ref_1k_-23.wav         I   -23.01 LUFS  peak  -20.00 dBTP
    ref_short_02.wav       I     -inf LUFS  peak   -6.02 dBTP   <-- 0.2 s clip
    ref_pink.wav           I    -8.92 LUFS  peak    0.82 dBTP

ffmpeg's ebur128 on the same files: -23.0 / -70.0 / -8.9 LUFS.

The two long files agree to 0.01 LU. The 0.2 s clip is where they differ, and
NEITHER number is a measurement: ffmpeg reports the -70 floor as a level, and
ebur128 returns -inf through gated_loudness_multiple's NoBlocksAboveThreshold arm.
relative_threshold() returning exactly -70.0 with no blocks is the same value a
genuine -70 LUFS passage produces, so #21 must count gating blocks rather than
trust loudness_global().
