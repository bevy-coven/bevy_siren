**Item**: `Rng::new(seed)` used the seed as the xorshift64 state, unscrambled. **Change**: the state is `splitmix64(seed)` (a Weyl step and Stafford's variant-13 finalizer); the generator, `seed()`, `ZERO_SEED`, the RON schema and `0` as an alias of the default are unchanged; the one seed that maps to the zero state plays the default stream; `model::splitmix64` is new and public. Every seeded stream changes once, and `needs-decision/seed-scramble.md` says so.

**What M4 – M6 say**: a statistical test cannot see a mixer that is weaker but adequate; only the per-bit avalanche and the known answers can, and the known answers are the ones that make a change to *this* stream a decision. Both exist for that reason, and `the_stream_is_the_one_the_docs_describe`'s comment says so.

## Pass 1 — correctness and contracts

*Critique.* (1) **Every place a stream is built or drawn from** — `grep` for `Rng::new(`, `next_u32`, `next_f32` over `src/` and `examples/`: `SirenChoices::new` (builds, and records `rng.seed()` — the built-from seed, which is what `seed_choices` compares), `VariantPicker::pick` and `pick_from` (draw), `play_requests` (the detune draw), `examples/render.rs:67` (the `variants …` report lines). None reads the state. (2) **The seed that maps to a zero state** — the mixer is a bijection with 0 ↦ 0, so exactly one seed gives xorshift's one fixed point; it plays the default stream, which makes `0`, `ZERO_SEED` and `0x61C8864680B583EB` one stream wearing three names. That is now in the guide, in the `Rng::new` doc and in the decision file, where the first draft said "any number is a good seed" with no exception. (3) **`seed()` must not report the state**: M2 is the proof it is a contract and not a style. (4) **The constants**: the Python oracle reproduces SplitMix64's published outputs from state 0 (`e220a8397b1dcdaf`, `6e789e6aa1b965f4`, `06c45d188009454f`, `f88bb8a8724c81ec`, `1b39896a51a8749b`) — a 64-bit match cannot be chance — and the same constants are in `symphonia-core` 0.5.5 and `isomesh` 0.0.11. (5) The oracle is **independent of the Rust code and not of me**: it checks the arithmetic, not that SplitMix64 is the right choice; that rests on Brent §4 (a non-linear step before a linear generator) and the measurements. (6) **The generator is unchanged**: a linear generator's long-stream artefacts (Blackman and Vigna 2021) are not measured as a defect of this crate and are carried off, not claimed fixed.
*Verdict:* **GREAT.** The measured defect is gone at its root and in one place, the contract that is easy to break (`seed()`) is pinned by twelve tests, and what the change does *not* do is written down.

## Pass 2 — tests: would they catch a plausible regression?

*Critique.* (1) **Red first, and for the right reason**: seven of the eight new tests fail on the unchanged tree with the numbers the probe printed (`[3000, 0]`, `r = +0.999`, *"bit 0 changes 0.00 of 32"*, *"1953 triples"*, play `[300, 0, 0]`); the eighth (`splitmix64_gives_the_reference_outputs`) could not exist before the function. (2) **The mutations above**: each guard has a test that names it and no test is redundant with another *for the same mutation* except the two known-answer tests, which are meant to be. (3) **Deterministic**: every seed is fixed, so a statistical result is a number that clears its bound or does not; the bounds (10.83 / 13.82 / 16.27 / 24.32 / 37.70, \|r\| < 4/√2999, 16.0 ± 0.1, [15, 17]) were written before the after-run and the 0.1 % points were verified numerically. (4) **The play-level test was too expensive and has been cut**: one app, 600 reseeds, 0.3 s (see the scorecard). (5) **What the thin margin means**: draw 12's \|r\| = 0.053 against 0.073; the test is a regression guard for this mixer, not a certificate for any mixer. (6) **No test of the render path** — and none needed: the WAVs are byte-identical and `render.txt`'s three `variants …` lines are the only change (they print the picks of `Rng::new(settings.seed)`).
*Verdict:* **GREAT**, after the rewrite of the play test; **NOT YET** at the first cut of it, which the mixer step's wall time failed.

## Catches this iteration (each cost a re-run or a rewrite)

1. **The mixer step's wall time** (13.21 s against under 2 s): a test that builds an app per case opens a device per case under `--features mixer`. The first cut was 300 apps (1.9 s in the default build), cut to 120 (0.83 s) *for that cost* — and the cost model was *apps*, where it should have been *apps with a device*. Found by reading the **time** of a green matrix; fixed by one reseeded app.
2. **A grep filter that dropped the probe's section headers** — the first read of the probe's output lost its `==` lines; re-run, the full output kept in `evidence/`.
3. **The disk**: `cargo build --examples --all-features` failed to link at 3.6 GB free and passed at 4.0 GB (14.4 s). **[INFERENCE] the disk and not the code**, said as such in `baseline-matrix.log`. Stale `scratch_*` binaries of a deleted example were removed by hand (all mine).
4. **Stale words** (pass 3, above), and 120's two wrong projections (the scorecard's first rows).
5. **A comment in the public rustdoc** that said *"this machine's crate registry"* — found by reading the diff in pass 3, not by any tool: the words were true and meant nothing to a reader of docs.rs.
