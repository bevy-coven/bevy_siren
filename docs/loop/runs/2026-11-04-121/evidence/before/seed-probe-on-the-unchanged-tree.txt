What this shows (iteration 120 measured one corner of it - 60 seeds, one play each; this is the whole shape):
 1. FOR EVERY SEED FROM 1 TO 3000 THE FIRST PICK OF A CUE IS ITS FIRST VARIANT, whatever the variant count (k = 2, 3, 4, 8: 3000 of 3000, chi-square = 3000 x (k - 1), p = 0). The first draw of every seed up to 4096 is in the lowest sixteenth
    of [0, 1): `Rng::new(1).next_u32()` is exactly 0x00000000. README: "two seeds are two streams from the first play"; docs/guide/cues.md: "Two projects that differ only in `seed` play different variants and different detunes from the first play".
 2. NEIGHBOURING SEEDS ARE NEIGHBOURING DRAWS: r = 0.9995 between the first draws of seed s and s+1 (independence would be within +-0.055).
 3. THE FIRST DRAW DOES NOT SEE THE LOW BITS OF THE SEED: flipping seed bit 0 or 1 changes none of the 32 output bits, bits 2-5 change one; the mean is 3.8 of 32 (a good mixer: 16).
 4. STREAMS FROM RELATED SEEDS ARE EXACTLY LINEAR IN EACH OTHER, FOR EVER: stream(a xor b) == stream(a) xor stream(b) draw for draw over the first 1000 draws, for 1953 of 1953 triples. Seeds 1, 2 and 3 are not three streams: the third is the first
    xor the second. This is Brent 2007 section 4 ("clearly undesirable"): xorshift is linear over GF(2), so the seeds of a linear generator must not be related by xor.
 5. IT IS NOT ONLY THE FIRST DRAW: draw n of seed s and of seed s+1 stay correlated at r = +0.65 (n = 2), -0.47, -0.60, -0.78 (n = 6), -0.46, -0.28, +0.50 (n = 24), -0.43, +0.40 (n = 64) - far outside +-0.055.
 6. Seeds with MANY ones are degenerate too (`Rng::new(u64::MAX).next_u32() == 0`). The default seed (0x5EED51AE00000001: 20 of 64 bits set) draws b42b988d, 58628b1d, 490ed9cc, e9e82178 first - unremarkable; its neighbourhood was not measured. [INFERENCE] that this is why the crate's own
    default-seeded renders and its tests (which use seeds 3, 5, 7, 11 and assert properties, not values) showed nothing.
