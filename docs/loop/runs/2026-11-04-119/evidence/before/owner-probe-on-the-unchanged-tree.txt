Reading it: four defects, one cause — `Play::on` parents the voice with `ChildOf(owner)`.
 1. A PANIC in Bevy's command handler when the owner is allocated and not yet spawned (`unordered`, `pending`): the relationship hook queues a command on an entity that does not exist yet.
 2. A placed sound is at the owner's transform composed with `.at(world)`: (200, 0, 0) for an owner at (100, 0, 0), and (0, 0, 0) — the listener's own position — for an owner with no Transform.
    The crate's level is computed for 100 m: Played.gain_db -27.96 dB and a compensation of +80.00 dB that divides out the panner's min(1, 1/d^2) at 100 m (rodio-0.22.2 source/spatial.rs:63). The panner sees 200 m — 12.04 dB quieter than
    the crate meant (20 log10((100/200)^2), by arithmetic from the measured positions) — or ~0 m, where min(1, 1/d^2) is 1 and the +80 dB stands: the sink is written -27.96 + 80.00 = +52.04 dB, 400 times the sample, where the law says -27.96 dB.
 3. An owner that was gone before the play: a voice with no ChildOf, a loop that lives for ever, the log saying only `played`.
 4. A log line that is false: the game despawned the voice, the owner is alive, the log says `owner despawned`.
