developer would type, and then measures Querybank normalisation over the crate's own 48-description bank.

== why: one class is near everything, and what normalising would change

  first place, by class: spaceEngineSmall × 3   explosionCrunch × 1   laserRetro × 1   spaceEngineLarge × 1   
  bank: 48 descriptions
  encoded 48 of 48 bank descriptions

  hit@1 over the bank: 9 of 48 raw, 14 of 48 normalised (19 % -> 29 %)

  Of the six descriptions above, 2 found their class. The bank figure is the one that means something:
  48 descriptions is a sample, and normalising lifted hit@1 by half — the direction the golden set measured
  (31 % -> 44.8 % on this library).
  The crate does NOT ship this: it needs an index-time statistic and a schema the editor reads, and the bank
  here is the set's own descriptions, which is the weakness the guide names. It is a direction, not a feature.
